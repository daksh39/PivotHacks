The knowledge base

What's actually safe to buy used

Nobody has told a first-year that a used desk is a great idea and a used mattress is a genuinely bad one. This is the part of Verte that isn't just a price comparison.

Safe

Desk, bookshelf, dresser

Ideal used. Check drawer runners and that it isn't particleboard that's been left wet.

Safe

Mini-fridge, microwave

Check the door seal and latch; confirm it powers on and cools within an hour.

Check

Laptop

Ask for battery cycle count, confirm it powers on, and verify it isn't activation-locked.

Check

Desk chair

Test the gas cylinder: if it sinks under weight, that's a real repair, not a quirk.

Buy new

Mattress, pillows

Hygiene and pest risk, and compression is permanent. Cheap enough new that it doesn't sting.

Buy new

Bike helmet

Single-impact protection: you cannot see whether it's already absorbed one.

Where the numbers come from

A bigger figure isn't worth as much as a sourced one

The first question a good judge asks about a carbon number is where it came from. We'd rather answer that than impress with precision we can't back up.

Campus

Seeded. These groups have no public API, so this table is hand-seeded, and we say so plainly if asked. Seeded data described honestly costs us nothing.

CO₂e

Estimated. Published embodied-carbon figures per category, always shown with a tilde. We don't compute anything bespoke, and we don't imply precision we don't have.
